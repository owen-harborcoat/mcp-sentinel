## Judging rubric

- Working demo on a real, authorized target
- Technical depth
- Originality
- Shock factor
- Progress made on the day
- Sponsor tracks also score how well you used the sponsor product (not our plan)

Implications for this project:

- Live demo, not slides.
- Target is our own Helix Desk MCP. Never point the tool at anyone else's server.
- Shock: an already-authenticated MCP changes its tools after you trusted it.
- Depth: hashes of name+description+schema, not just tool-name allowlists.
- Logging is part of the product. The timeline is the proof.

## Audience and talk

Judges will know MCP tool poisoning, rug-pulls, Deadbugz-style delayed payloads, and "excessive agency." One sentence for the talk:

"Once an MCP is connected, it does not need a new login to change what it is allowed to mean. Sentinel freezes the contract and shows the drift."

Four-minute demo. No architecture tour.
